I am Yen-Hsiang (Robert) Huang, a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/) and a graduate research assistant with [SCCAD (former GTCAD)](https://sites.usc.edu/sccad/), advised by [Prof. Sung Kyu Lim](http://limsk.ece.gatech.edu/).

My research focuses on electronic design automation, physical design, 3D integrated circuits, differentiable optimization, and hardware synthesis and verification.

## Research Experience

### [SCCAD (former GTCAD)](https://sites.usc.edu/sccad/), Georgia Tech

Graduate Research Assistant · September 2022–Present

- **3D IC Optimization through 3D-via Reduction and Inter-die Cell Relocation:** timing optimization through reduced critical-path snaking and inter-die cell relocation.
- **SRC JUMP 2.0 CHIMES:** reduced worst negative slack (WNS) of legacy-node 3D ICs by 50–93% compared with the state of the art, achieving 87.9–97.7% of advanced-node 2D performance.
- **Die Bonding Bumps and Pads Legalization:** developed a 3D-via legalization stage with refinement and eliminated all 3D-via overlaps below 40% via utilization.

## Work Experience

### Synopsys Inc. — R&D Engineer

Synopsys Headquarters · May 2025–December 2025

- Researched AI-based signal-integrity analysis for 2.5D ICs using graph neural networks to predict final-stage SI performance from early-stage design data.
- Independently implemented two provided prototypes in C++ and merged the resulting code into the main branch.

### Synopsys Inc., Concurrent Clock-and-Data (CCD) Team — Software Engineer R&D Intern

Hsinchu, Taiwan · July 2021–August 2021

- Enhanced the flip-flop merging algorithm in Synopsys IC Compiler II by considering flip-flop timing slack.
- Improved timing quality across 13 industrial benchmarks, achieving 5.14%/3.47% improvements in worst/total negative slack.

### Cadence Inc., Conformal Team — Software Engineer R&D Intern

Hsinchu, Taiwan · June 2020–September 2020

- Developed electronic change-order test cases as part of a five-person team for Cadence Conformal robustness testing.
- Generated more than 2,000 cases across five categories in which Conformal behaved unusually and proposed three improved flows to address them.
