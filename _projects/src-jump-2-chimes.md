As part of the Semiconductor Research Corporation (SRC) JUMP 2.0 CHIMES program, this work addressed physical-design quality in legacy-node 3D ICs.

The project reduced worst negative slack (WNS) by 50–93% compared with the state of the art, achieving 87.9–97.7% of advanced-node 2D performance.
