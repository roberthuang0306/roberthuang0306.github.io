I am Yen-Hsiang (Robert) Huang, a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology and a graduate research assistant in the GTCAD Lab, advised by Prof. Sung Kyu Lim.

My research focuses on electronic design automation, physical design, 3D integrated circuits, differentiable optimization, and hardware synthesis and verification.

Research Experience

GTCAD Lab., Georgia Tech

Graduate Research Assistant · September 2022–Present

  • 3D IC Optimization through 3D-via Reduction and Inter-die Cell Relocation: timing optimization through reduced critical-path snaking and inter-die cell relocation.
  • SRC JUMP 2.0 CHIMES: reduced worst negative slack (WNS) of legacy-node 3D ICs by 50–93% compared with the state of the art, achieving 87.9–97.7% of advanced-node 2D performance.
  • Die Bonding Bumps and Pads Legalization: developed a 3D-via legalization stage with refinement and eliminated all 3D-via overlaps below 40% via utilization.
