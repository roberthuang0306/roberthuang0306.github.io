    Research Profile

    I am a Ph.D. Student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology and a graduate research assistant with SCCAD (former GTCAD), advised by Prof. Sung Kyu Lim.

    Research

    My work focuses on electronic design automation, physical design, and 3D integrated circuits.

    • 3D IC optimization: 3D-via reduction, inter-die cell relocation, and timing optimization.
    • SRC JUMP 2.0 CHIMES: reducing the performance gap between legacy-node 3D ICs and advanced-node 2D ICs.
    • Die-bonding bump and pad legalization: refined 3D-via legalization with no overlaps below 40% via utilization.

    Work Experience

    • Synopsys Inc., Synopsys Headquarters — R&D Engineer (May–December 2025): researched AI-based signal-integrity analysis for 2.5D ICs using graph neural networks to predict final-stage SI performance from early-stage design data; independently implemented two provided prototypes in C++ and merged the resulting code into the main branch.
    • Synopsys Inc., CCD Team — Software Engineer R&D Intern (July–August 2021): enhanced flip-flop merging in IC Compiler II by considering timing slack and improved worst/total negative slack by 5.14%/3.47% across 13 industrial benchmarks.
    • Cadence Inc., Conformal Team — Software Engineer R&D Intern (June–September 2020): generated more than 2,000 ECO cases and proposed three improved Conformal flows.
